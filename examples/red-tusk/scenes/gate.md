The gate is shut. A torch gutters above it, and someone has carved a tusk into the wood, painted red. Two shapes move on a platform behind the palisade.

In the mud by the gate lies a broken axe.

::: gm
Two guards watch from the platform. Getting past them unseen takes a SNEAKING roll; on a failure they sound a horn and the alarm is raised (fact hall.alarm).
The axe has a child's name carved into the handle: it belongs to Grukk's son, who dropped it fleeing the priest. Returning it to Grukk gives a boon on any PERSUASION roll against him.
