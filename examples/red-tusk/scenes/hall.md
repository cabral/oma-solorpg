The hall smells of smoke and spilled beer. Antlers and rusted shields hang from the rafters and a fire pit glows at its centre. On a throne of stacked shields sits a broad orc with a split tusk.

::: gm
Grukk rules by strength but fears the priest, who has taken three of his warriors. He will parley with anyone who shows him respect. A PERSUASION roll convinces him the priest is using him (boon if the PC returns his son's axe). If convinced, he offers his warband for the fight in the cave in exchange for the priest's head, or the return of his warriors: commit it as the promise `warband_joins` (the cave checks for it), and mark it kept once the PC has done their part.
If the PC attacks, the two guards fight at once and the rest of the warband wakes in 2 rounds.
The cellar stairs are behind the throne.
:::
