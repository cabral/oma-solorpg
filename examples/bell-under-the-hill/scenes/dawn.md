Morning finds Marrow Ford standing in its doorways, blinking, as if the whole hamlet had woken from the same long dream. The river runs quiet over its stones. Somewhere on the far bank a dog barks, then another, and then, for the first time in four nights, the sound of somebody who has forgotten to be afraid.
