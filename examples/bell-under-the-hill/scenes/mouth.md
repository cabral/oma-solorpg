The barrow is a long green swell of hill with a mouth of two great stones and a lintel across them, and the door is a slab of stone that has been dragged open the width of a body. Three lamp-posts stand before it in a row, blacker than the others, their cups gaping. Beyond the slab is a passage going down into a cold that smells of clay and old cloth.

Bare footprints go in. None come out.

::: gm
**The three posts before the door** are the barrow's lamps. If the hero has the oil (from Osk, Brenna or their own supply) and lights them, commit the fact **`lamps.lit`**. Speaking the names as they do it gives the whole rite a chance; the dead will still be here, but Hild will know, and what waits inside behaves differently. To do this in the dark, with a bell tolling, is *Secure an Advantage* (+heart or +wits, the hero's choice and story), or *Face Danger* if the bell tolls while they're at it. Each lamp is a beat: don't roll for all three; roll once for the act, and fill it with what the hero does.

**Nothing is inside the mouth yet.** The passage runs down forty paces to the long hall. It's cold, and the footprints go all the way.

**Moves that fit here:** Secure an Advantage (lighting the lamps, making a plan, offering bread at the door), Face Danger (the passage in the dark), Gather Information (the footprints; the carvings on the lintel: Hild's names, worn but readable), Forge a Bond (if a companion or an ally has come this far).
:::
