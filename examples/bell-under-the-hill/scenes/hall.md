The long hall runs into the hill, low and narrow, its walls cut into niches that hold the barrow's dead in linen the colour of old teeth. At the far end an iron-bound door stands shut, and between the hero and it a rope hangs down through a hole in the roof: rough, damp, and moving, as if something above were pulling it.

Three living people stand in a ring under the rope in their nightclothes. One is a boy. They take the rope in turns, and pull, and above the roof a bell answers. Their eyes are open and wet and they do not see you.

Around them, at every niche, the linen stirs.

::: gm
**The ringers** are Wen, old Marta and Egil, alive and lost (they are NPCs too, so the hero can learn their names). They will not answer, but they can be led by the hand if the bell stops, or turned by a firm touch if the hero is quick. They are *not* foes: harming them in the fight is a terrible thing to have done.

**The hollow ones** are the barrow's guardians, dead who hold the hall: three of them for now, more if the bell clock has passed 4. They rise from the niches when the hero steps past the third: gaunt shapes, cloth-wrapped, quick. Treat them as **one fight**, a pack of foes at rank *dangerous* (2 progress per harm; they inflict 2 harm). Begin with *Enter the Fray* (+heart if the hero faces them, +shadow if the hero moves in unseen, +wits if ambushed), start a track: **`solo track add "The hollow ones" --kind combat --rank dangerous`**, and run the fight with Strike and Clash. Each harm the hero inflicts is a mark on the track (`solo track mark the_hollow_ones --times <harm>`), and when the hero makes a decisive move on a strong hit they may *End the Fight*: a progress roll against the track. If lamps are lit (`lamps.lit`), the hollow ones are hesitant: give the hero +1 on their first move in the fight, and each hollow one that falls does so quietly.

A sneak past them is possible with *Face Danger* (+shadow): they aren't looking for the living so much as for the rite left undone.

**The iron door** is barred from the far side, and opens when the hollow ones are dealt with, or when Hild allows it. Commit **`door.open`** when the hero can go through. On the far side is the bell.

**Free the ringers** with a strong hit on *Face Danger* (+iron, hauling them from the rope) or after the bell falls silent. When they are free, they can be led out; commit them safe in a note. Marta, if freed, can say the rest of the rite: *she* knows what Hild wants, and will say it if she wakes.

**Moves that fit here:** Enter the Fray, Strike, Clash, End the Fight, Endure Harm, Face Danger, Secure an Advantage, Turn the Tide (once, if the fight turns).
