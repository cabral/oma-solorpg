Marrow Ford is twelve turf-roofed houses where the river Skarn spreads wide over its gravel and the old road wades across. A mill stands on the near bank. A flat ferry lies drawn up on the shingle, and beside it an old man sits on an upturned boat, facing the water. It is dusk, and nobody is working. The doors are barred. The dogs lie with their chins flat on the ground.

Then, from the hill behind the hamlet, a bell tolls. One slow stroke. Every head turns toward it, and a woman at the well makes a sign against harm with a hand that isn't steady.

::: gm
**What is happening.** For three nights, at moonrise, a bell has tolled from the barrow on Hallow Hill. Each night someone has got up out of their bed, barefoot and with open eyes, and walked up the hill road: Wen the miller's boy the first night, old Marta the midwife the second, Egil the shepherd the third. Nobody has come back. Marrow Ford has no fighters left (the young ones went to a war three winters ago) and no one to send.

**The truth** (it comes out through play, in pieces): the bell belongs to Hild, the Warden, who was laid in the hill three hundred winters ago on an oath. So long as the people of the ford lit her three lamps at midwinter and said her names, she would keep the ford's dead from walking. This winter Osk, the elder, let the lamps go dark: oil is dear, and the young ones laughed at the custom. Hild is not cruel. She is calling the oathbreakers to account, and she has taken the sleepwalkers to ring her bell, because a bell must be rung.

**How to begin.** The hero arrives at dusk, for whatever reason the player gives. Let them look around and meet people; nobody in Marrow Ford will ask them to be a hero, and everyone will let them. Tam, on the boat, will talk if spoken to. When the player is ready, offer the vow: *Swear an Iron Vow* (+heart) to silence the bell and bring the missing home. Rank it dangerous. If they hold back, let the bell toll again and let the woman at the well say Wen's name. A hero who never swears can still play; they simply have nothing to fulfill at the end.

**Moves that fit here:** Swear an Iron Vow, Gather Information (Tam, the well, the barred doors), Compel (a frightened hamlet), Forge a Bond (later, once they've done something for someone), Resupply (the hamlet has little, and will share what it has). A bond with Marrow Ford itself is possible after the hero has kept them safe a night; ask the oracle.

Ask the oracle whenever you aren't sure what the hero finds, and write anything that matters into the story (facts, notes).
